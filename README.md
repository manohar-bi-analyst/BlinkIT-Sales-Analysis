# BlinkIT-Sales-Analysis

Problem Statement:
Retail grocery chains like BlinkIT generate large volumes of transactional data across outlets, item categories, and customer segments. However, raw data alone does not provide actionable insights.
This project aims to analyze BlinkIT’s sales performance using SQL, Power BI, and presentation storytelling to uncover key business drivers and support data‑driven decision making.

Approach:

 SQL Analysis:
      Wrote queries to calculate total sales by fat content, item type, outlet size, outlet location type, and establishment year.
      Derived percentage contributions and pivoted data for comparative insights.

Power BI Dashboard:
      Built interactive visuals for sales by outlet size, outlet type, item category, and fat content.
      Added KPIs for total sales, average sales, item count, and customer ratings.
      Enabled filters for outlet size and type to allow dynamic exploration.

 Presentation (PPT):
      Summarized findings into clear business insights with supporting charts.
      Highlighted leading segments, outlet performance, and customer satisfaction trends.

Key Insights

Fat Content:
Low‑fat products dominate sales (≈ 644K), outperforming regular fat items (≈ 352K).

Item Categories:
Fruits & Vegetables, Snack Foods, and Household items are the top three categories, driving majority sales.

Outlet Location:
Tier 2 and Tier 3 outlets are the sales engine, contributing ~393K and ~341K respectively.

Outlet Size:
Medium and small outlets account for most sales, showing higher efficiency compared to large outlets.

Outlet Format:
Supermarket Type1 leads with ~787K sales, far ahead of other formats.

Customer Ratings:
Ratings are stable across formats (3.95–3.97), with Grocery Stores slightly higher at 3.97.

Establishment Year:
Sales remain broadly stable across outlet establishment years, ranging between ~78K and ~133K.
